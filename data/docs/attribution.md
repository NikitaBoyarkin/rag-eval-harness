# Attribution

Attribution assigns revenue or conversions to the marketing channel that produced them. First-touch attribution credits the channel that first brought the user; last-touch credits the channel of the most recent interaction before conversion. First-touch rewards demand generation; last-touch rewards closing. Multi-touch models spread credit across the path, time-decay giving more to recent touches. The choice changes which channel looks best: a channel that brings one-and-done purchasers will look strong under first-touch but weak on lifetime value. The gap between first-touch and lifetime revenue is itself a signal: large gaps mark channels with repeat-purchase potential worth investing in. There is no single correct model; the rule is to pick one and apply it consistently.
