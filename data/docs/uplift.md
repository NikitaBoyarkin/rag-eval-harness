# Uplift Modeling

Uplift modeling estimates the individual treatment effect (ITE): how much a treatment moves the outcome for a specific user, not on average. A T-learner fits one model per treatment arm and takes uplift as the difference of the two predictions, P(treatment) - P(control). An S-learner fits a single model with the treatment flag as a feature and computes uplift as the prediction with treatment=1 minus treatment=0. The models are ranked by AUUC (area under the uplift curve), the Qini coefficient, and uplift at top-k: if you act on the top 20 percent scored by uplift, how much extra response do you get. Uplift answers a different question than A/B testing: not whether the treatment works on average, but who it works on, so actions target the users who actually respond.
