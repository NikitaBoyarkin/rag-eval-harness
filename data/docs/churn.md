# Churn Prediction

Churn prediction models which users will stop being active. The label is typically defined as no activity in a future window, such as the next 30 days, for users who were active in the prior 30 days (real at-risk customers). The central failure mode is leakage: features must be computed as-of the snapshot date and the train, validation, and test split must be chronological by snapshot, not random, so that future activity never leaks into training. Recency (days since last activity) is usually the strongest feature. The business metric is recall at top decile and lift at top decile: if retention acts on the riskiest 10 percent of users, how many actual churners are caught. ROC-AUC measures ranking quality; Brier score measures calibration, which matters when scores drive spend.
